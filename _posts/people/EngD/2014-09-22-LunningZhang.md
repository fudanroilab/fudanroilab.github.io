Lunning Zhang is currently an EngD student at the College of Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence, Fudan University, supervised by Prof. Wenqiang Zhang. His research interests are embodied-intelligence robotic systems, vision-language-action (VLA) models and vision-language navigation (VLN). During his master's study at Xinjiang Agricultural University, he was awarded the National Graduate Scholarship. His work on spatio-temporal intelligence has received four provincial and ministerial science and technology awards, including the Science and Technology Progress Award (First Prize) of Xinjiang Uygur Autonomous Region.
